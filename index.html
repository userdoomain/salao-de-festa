Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Chat to us on WhatsApp.

How does date reservation work?

You book a visit, choose your package and pay a 30% deposit to secure the date. The balance can be paid in up to 12 interest-free instalments.

Can I bring my own caterer and decorator?

Yes. Vasa Aurora has a list of trusted partners, but you're free to bring your own suppliers — the team is happy to make recommendations.

Is the venue accessible?

We have access ramps, adapted restrooms and reserved parking. The hall is fully accessible for wheelchair users and those with reduced mobility.

Is set-up time included?

It depends on the package: with Essence, set-up is on the day; with Primavera and Aurora, you have the afternoon before to decorate at your leisure.

Shall we plan it together?

Book a visit, request a quote or ask us anything. We'll reply within one business day.
