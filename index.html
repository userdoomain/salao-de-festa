Common questions

Didn't find what you're looking for? Talk to us on WhatsApp.

How does booking a date work?

You schedule a visit, choose your package and pay a 30% deposit to secure the date. The remainder can be paid in up to 12 interest-free installments.

Can I bring my own caterer and decorator?

Yes. Vasa Aurora works with a list of partners, but you're free to hire whichever providers you prefer — our team is available for recommendations.

Is the space accessible?

We have access ramps, adapted restrooms and reserved parking spaces. The hall is fully accessible for wheelchair users and people with reduced mobility.

Is setup time included?

It depends on the package: with Essence, setup is done on the day; with Primavera and Aurora, you have the afternoon before to decorate at your own pace.

Let's plan together?
